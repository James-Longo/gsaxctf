Some of these results are in PDF format and require Adobe Acrobat Reader to view.

How to send results:  Meet directors and coaches are encouraged to send results to be posted on the website.  We will accept results in the following formats:  an HTML report from a meet management program (such as Hy-Tek Meet Manager or RunScore), Excel, Word, PDF or text files. Do not send hand-written, scanned files because they are hard to read and the file sizes are too large.  Send the results as an e-mail attachment to Glendon Rand at [email protected].
